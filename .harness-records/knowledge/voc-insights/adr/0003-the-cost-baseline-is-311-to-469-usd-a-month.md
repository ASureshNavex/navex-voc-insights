# ADR 0003: the cost baseline is 311 to 469 USD a month

Status: accepted. Date: 2026-11-19.

## Context
The deck estimates 1,278 USD a month for the full roadmap; the newer cost workbook estimates 311.15 for the MVP and 468.65 with Phase 1 and 2 (sections/22).

## Decision
The planning baseline is the workbook: 311 USD a month for the MVP, 469 with Phase 1 and 2. The deck's 1,278 is superseded.

## Consequences
Sizing and budget follow the workbook's per-environment figures. They are us-east-1 list prices (ADR 0002).
