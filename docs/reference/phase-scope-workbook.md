## Target State scope
| Capability | Who is involved | What it does and how it is used | Status and what NAVEX needs to confirm | Comments |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Enterprise customer intelligence | VoC program owners and department leads | Extend access to customer intelligence beyond CXM and Product. Identify the recurring questions each department should be able to answer, the reports or views it needs and which evidence its users may access. | Target State direction. Confirm rollout order, named audiences and minimum capabilities for each. | To be discussed in detail |
| Account Executive and Sales leadership views | Account Executives and Sales leaders | Give AEs visibility into the friction themes and relevant customer context for their assigned accounts. Give Sales leaders a team-level view to identify accounts needing a discussion or support. | Discussed. Confirm phase placement, account ownership mapping, visible fields and allowed detail. | TBD |
| Executive and Finance reporting | Executive leadership and Finance | Provide an agreed summary of cross-account or cross-product themes for recurring leadership and Finance questions. Use drill-down or supporting evidence where appropriate to explain what is driving a trend. | Discussed. Confirm actual questions, approved metrics, report cadence and access before defining the output. | TBD |
| Four-quadrant Fact Base report | Product Operations and Product Managers | Recreate the monthly Product intelligence report by domain. Bring together customer evidence, internal signals such as support themes, market insights and competitive changes. Product Managers can review the report and select evidence relevant to their strategy. | Target State. Confirm domain coverage, how high-signal domains are selected, and inputs for each quadrant. | TBD |
| Living Product Fact Base | Product Managers | Support adding selected evidence to the standing Fact Base for a product. For example, retain an interview finding that helps explain a broader product strategy decision. The Product Manager decides which evidence is included. | Target State candidate. Confirm the application workflow and how it connects to the existing living artifact in Aha!. | TBD |
| Initiative Fact Base | Product Managers and initiative owners | Support attaching relevant evidence to a specific roadmap initiative. For example, associate customer quotes with an access improvement so a reviewer can see why that initiative was proposed. | Target State candidate. Confirm how the VoC application links evidence to the existing initiative record in Aha! and who may make changes. | TBD |
| Aha! idea votes and comments | Product Operations and Product Managers | Enrich product ideas with available votes, comments, proxy votes and associated opportunity value. Show the added context alongside the idea so Product can see the demand behind it. | Target State. Confirm which fields Aha! can provide, how records are matched and who can see sales opportunity values. | TBD |
| Aha! evidence and commentary handoff | Product Managers | Let an authorized PM select evidence or a note in VoC and identify the Product or initiative Fact Base it belongs to in Aha!. Preserve the source context when handing it off. | Target State candidate. Confirm whether the user opens a link, copies evidence or writes back directly, and which destinations are permitted. | TBD |
| Competitive and market intelligence | Product Operations and Product Managers | Bring relevant competitive or market developments from Klue or its approved successor into the Fact Base. Associate each item with the relevant product domain and retain its source and reporting period. | Target State. Confirm vendor, data access and domain mapping before committing the interface. | TBD |
| Call transcripts and additional sources | VoC model owner and Product Operations | Evaluate whether call transcripts or other new sources add useful context and how they affect friction ratings. Compare sample outputs before and after adding each source, then decide where it can be used. | Phase placement to confirm after evaluation. Agree when sources may appear as evidence and when they may influence scoring. | TBD |
| Decision support and department champions | Business leads and VoC program owners | Identify the decision each audience needs to make from VoC, the evidence it needs to make it and who owns adoption in each department. For example, Product may review a recurring issue during roadmap planning. | Later-phase direction. Confirm specific use cases, owners and release placement with NAVEX. | TBD |
